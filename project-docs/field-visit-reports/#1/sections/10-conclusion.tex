\section*{Conclusion}
The visit to Falcon Tea Factory confirmed that there is a market demand for software solutions within the tea industry. Although the factory already utilizes management software, its current system focuses primarily on interactions between Tea Estate Owners and Factory Administration. The proposed Tea Collection Management System aims to provide a more comprehensive solution by connecting multiple stakeholders throughout the tea collection process. Further field visits are necessary to understand varying factory requirements and ensure that the proposed system addresses industry-wide needs effectively.
