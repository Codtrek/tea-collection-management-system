A field visit was conducted to Falcon Tea Factory to gain an understanding of the factory's operational processes, existing software solutions, and potential software requirements. The visit provided valuable insights into how technology is currently being utilized within the tea manufacturing industry and helped identify opportunities for the proposed Tea Collection Management System.
