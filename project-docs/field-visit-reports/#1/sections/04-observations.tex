\section*{Observations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Falcon Tea Factory has adopted software solutions for several management activities, with some overlap to the proposed application features.
  \item The factory owner has a Civil Engineering background and shows a strong interest in technology and innovation, resulting in investment in modern machinery and advanced solutions.
  \item The factory is more digitally mature than typical tea factories, with a strong emphasis on technological advancement and product quality.
  \item The current software primarily facilitates communication and operations between Tea Estate Owners and Factory Administration.
  \item A separate software system is used for financial management activities.
\end{itemize}
