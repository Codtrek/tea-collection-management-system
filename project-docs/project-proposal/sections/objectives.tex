\section{Objectives of the Project}
\label{sec:objectives}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Digitize estate-to-factory operations.} \\The system transforms manual tea collection, delivery, and payment workflows into a centralized digital platform for estate owners, managers, collection agents, and factories.
    \item \textbf{Reduce manual paperwork and errors.} \\Collection records, delivery confirmations, and payment deductions are stored digitally to minimize duplication and human error.
    \item \textbf{Improve verification and accountability.} \\OTP-based weight verification and complaint handling strengthen traceability and reduce disputes over delivery weights.
    \item \textbf{Enable fertilizer workflow management.} \\The system supports fertilizer requests, approvals, borrowing, transport tracking, and cost deductions.
    \item \textbf{Provide transparent reporting and analytics.} \\Dashboards and reports help stakeholders monitor collections, employee performance, and income generation.
    \item \textbf{Build a scalable platform.} \\The architecture supports future expansion to other agricultural collection workflows and additional users.
\end{itemize}
