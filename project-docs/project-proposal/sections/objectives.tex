\section{Objectives of the Project}
\label{sec:objectives}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Digitize agricultural collection management.} \\The system transforms the traditional manual tea collection process into a fully digital platform. Collection details, farmer information, delivery records, and payment data can be managed electronically, making the entire process faster, more organized, and easier to monitor.
    \item \textbf{Reduce manual paperwork.} \\Currently, tea collection activities rely heavily on paper-based records and handwritten notes. The proposed system reduces the use of physical documents by storing all collection and payment information digitally, minimizing errors and saving time for both collectors and factories.
    \item \textbf{Improve communication efficiency.} \\The platform improves communication between farmers, collectors, and factories through a centralized system. Important updates, collection schedules, and payment details can be shared quickly, reducing delays and misunderstandings caused by phone-based communication.
    \item \textbf{Provide transparent records for both farmers and collectors.} \\The system maintains accurate and accessible digital records for all users. Farmers and collectors can easily view collection histories, payment details, and transaction records, ensuring transparency and trust among all parties involved.
    \item \textbf{Reduce disputes caused by inconsistent records.} \\Manual record keeping often leads to missing or inaccurate information, causing disagreements regarding collections and payments. By maintaining consistent digital records, the system helps reduce disputes and improves accountability.
    \item \textbf{Build a scalable platform for future agricultural sectors.} \\Although the system is initially designed for tea collection management, the platform is developed with scalability in mind. In the future, it can be expanded and adapted to support other agricultural industries and collection processes across Sri Lanka.
\end{itemize}
