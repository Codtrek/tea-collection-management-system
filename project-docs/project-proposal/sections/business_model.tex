\subsection{Business Model}

The Tea Collection Management System operates as a centralized platform connecting tea estate owners
and factories, aiming to improve transparency and reduce communication gaps
that often lead to disputes or fraudulent practices in the tea collection
process.

\textbf{Value Proposition.} The platform offers a centralized system that
connects estate owners and factories, replacing fragmented or manual
processes with a single source of truth. By digitizing collection records,
payments, and fertilizer management, the system reduces errors and bugs
common in existing factory management workflows. A modern, intuitive user
interface improves usability over legacy systems, while increased
transparency between estate owners and factories helps minimize scams
arising from poor communication.

\textbf{Customer Segments.} The platform serves two main user groups: tea
estate owners, who supply collection data, and tea factories, who manage
and process that data at scale.

\textbf{Revenue Model.} The platform follows an asymmetric B2B subscription
model, where the side that benefits most financially bears the cost, while
the supplier side is onboarded at no cost to maximize adoption:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Estate Owners} -- free access at all times. Since
    full participation from estate owners is essential for the platform
    to function, no charges are applied to this user group. This
    encourages complete adoption and prevents reversion to manual,
    paper-based record-keeping.
    \item \textbf{Factories -- Standard Plan} -- LKR 10,000 per month,
    providing core features for managing collections, payments, and
    reporting.
    \item \textbf{Factories -- Pro Plan} -- LKR 15,000 per month,
    unlocking advanced features for larger-scale operations, effectively
    subsidizing the platform's overall cost structure.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Channels.} The system is accessible through a web-based admin
portal and a mobile application for field officers, with onboarding
supported through direct demonstrations to estates and factories.

\textbf{Key Activities and Resources.} Core activities include ongoing
platform development, infrastructure maintenance, and customer support.
Key resources include the development team, cloud hosting infrastructure,
and domain expertise in tea industry workflows.

\textbf{Cost Structure.} Primary costs consist of server and hosting
expenses, development and maintenance effort, and customer support,
covered entirely by factory subscription revenue rather than estate
owner contributions.
