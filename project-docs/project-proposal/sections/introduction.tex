\section{Introduction to the Project}
\label{sec:introduction}

The Tea Collection Management System is a digital platform developed to modernize tea leaf collection and distribution in Sri Lanka. Today, tea estate owners, managers, collection agents, receiving officers, and factories often rely on phone calls and paper-based records. This leads to inaccurate records, delayed communication, and difficulty tracking deliveries, payments, and complaints.

This system aims to simplify and digitize the end-to-end workflow from estate-level plucking records to factory delivery confirmation. Our startup company will provide a centralized and transparent platform that supports OTP-based weight verification, complaint handling, fertilizer requests, payment management, and operational reporting. The solution improves efficiency, accuracy, and accountability while reducing dependency on manual processes.
