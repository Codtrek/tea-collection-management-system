\section{Introduction to the Project}
\label{sec:introduction}

Even as the world rapidly embraces digital transformation, the tea industry still largely depends on manual paper-based records and phone calls to manage daily collection operations. This traditional approach often results in inaccurate record keeping, delayed communication, weight disputes, and limited transparency across the tea supply chain.

These challenges not only reduce operational efficiency but also create frustration and trust issues among estate owners, collection agents, receiving officers, and factories.

Recognizing these long-standing inefficiencies within one of Sri Lanka’s most significant industries, the Tea Collection Management System is proposed as a centralized digital solution aimed at modernizing and streamlining tea collection and distribution processes.

The proposed system is designed to replace fragmented manual practices with a transparent and technology-driven platform that aims to improve efficiency, accountability, accuracy, and trust while supporting the tea industry’s transition into the digital era.
