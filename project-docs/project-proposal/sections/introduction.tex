\section{Introduction to the Project}
\label{sec:introduction}

The Tea Collection Management System is a digital platform developed to modernize the traditional tea leaf collection process in Sri Lanka. Currently, farmers, collectors, and factories manage operations manually using phone calls and paper-based records. This creates issues such as inaccurate record keeping, delays in communication, and difficulties in tracking collections and payments.

This system aims to simplify and modernize the collection process of tea leaves. Our startup company aims to digitalize the entire tea collection process through a centralized and transparent platform. The system will support digital record management, worker payment calculations, and communication between farmers, collectors, and factories. This solution will improve efficiency, accuracy, and transparency while reducing dependency on manual processes.
